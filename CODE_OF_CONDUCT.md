# Code of conduct

We expect respectful, constructive participation. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and publication of another person's private data are not acceptable.

Focus criticism on ideas and code, assume good faith while still holding security and quality boundaries, and make room for different experience levels. Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or ban participation that harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private contact mechanisms. Do not disclose another person's identity or sensitive information in a public issue.
